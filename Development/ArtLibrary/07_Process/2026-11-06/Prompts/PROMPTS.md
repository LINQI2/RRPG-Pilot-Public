# ImageGen prompts and provenance

Date: 2026-11-06. Built-in image_gen tool; no fallback CLI/API subscription used. Concept boards only, not final production sprites. All three use generated new images, no copied reference-game assets.

## visual-target-01.png

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one polished art-direction review board for RRPG, an existing single-player village-community simulation plus expedition tactical card RPG. This is a PROPOSAL, not a final game screenshot.
Primary request: demonstrate one coherent, production-feasible art language across a lived-in village, ordinary adult inhabitants, battle and UI. Wide landscape art director presentation, crisp game illustration, exceptionally clear value hierarchy.
Layout: left 60% a LARGE actual gameplay-camera village view (oblique orthographic, approx 35 degrees looking down): five modest houses grouped into residential cluster, shared kitchen with chimney, compact crop beds as one agricultural block, tavern with plain pictorial hanging sign, quiet open communal ground. A few adult residents working/socializing. Paths only a subtle ground treatment. Read big spatial groups first, intermediate architectural detail second; broad quiet low-texture ground. No sprawling fantasy city. Upper right 40% an ordinary adult character lineup: a tall lean hunter, sturdy cook, older farmer, same drawing family and anatomically coherent approximately 6 to 6.5 heads tall, normal arm lengths, varied adult bodies, no chibi or toy bodies. Strong identity from silhouette, hairstyle, outfit and gesture. Lower right an inset BATTLE UI composition: two separate opposing 3-by-3 boards with a clear central gap, allies on left, enemies on right; visible readable occupancy, understated battlefield and a tidy row of 3 illustrated cards below. Portraits sit inside dedicated art wells, no text overlapping bodies.
Style: clean stylized 2D artwork with dimensional architectural planes and soft contact shading; substantial medium-scale shapes, crisp contours, restrained outline weights, rich but organized. Material detail expressed through structure, not noisy textures. Consistent lighting from upper left, broad readable shadow faces. Welcoming settlement plus adventurous wilderness mood, provisional modest low-fantasy vernacular with no imposed lore, no recognizable franchise assets.
Color: warm ivory walls, terracotta roofs, restrained deep teal UI framing, wheat gold accents, quiet light sage terrain, dark blue-grey text. Keep readable in grayscale; selection uses outline and a corner marker, never red/green alone.
UI: compact functional panels, spacious gutters, no excessive parchment, no ornate gold fantasy frame; small empty label fields rather than fake microtext. Board header exact text "RRPG / VISUAL TARGET 01"; section labels only "HOME", "PEOPLE", "BATTLE". No other text.
Avoid: pixel art, realistic photographic 3D, watercolor blur, gritty dirt overlays, toddler proportions, massive heads, oversized arms, crowds of identical residents, busy ground texture, cinematic camera with tiny unreadable units, lush illustration hiding interface, franchise logos.
The outcome must feel like a coherent achievable indie game visual target, not a splash illustration.
```
