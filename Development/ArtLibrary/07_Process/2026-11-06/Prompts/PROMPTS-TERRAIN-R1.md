Perfect straight-down orthographic material texture with NO baked perspective. Fill the entire square edge-to-edge with the same dirt material, seamless at all four edges. No actual path shape, no grass margins, no border, no edge fade, no gradient, no vignette, no shadows, no furrows, no wheel tracks, no deep cracks, no footprints, no objects, no plants, no buildings, no characters, no text, no grid. Material texture only, NOT an isometric tile sprite and NOT a scene.
